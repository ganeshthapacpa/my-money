FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is My Money really free?

Yes. Creating an account and using every feature costs nothing, and there are no ads.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You enter balances and log transactions yourself, which takes a few seconds each. Many people find that logging by hand makes them more aware of their spending.

How is "safe to spend" calculated?

It takes what's left in your everyday budgets this month and divides it by the days remaining, including today. Bills, transfers and card payments are kept separate so the number reflects day-to-day spending.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Open My Money in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu in Chrome, then Install app. It opens full screen like any other app.

Is my data safe?

Connections are encrypted, stored data is encrypted at rest, and database rules make sure each account can only see its own data. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Choose "Use without an account" and your data stays in that browser only. If you create an account later, your data moves into it automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings from the gear icon on the Home screen and choose Delete my account. Your account and all of its data are removed permanently.
