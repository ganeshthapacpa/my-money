FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is My Money free?

Yes. Creating an account and using every feature is free.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You enter balances and log transactions yourself, which takes a few seconds each. Many people find logging by hand makes them more aware of their spending.

Does it work on my iPhone or Android phone?

Yes. Open My Money in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu in Chrome, then Install app. It opens full screen like any other app.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Choose "Use without an account" and your data stays in that browser only. You can create an account later and your data moves into it automatically.

How do I record a credit card payment?

Add a Transfer from your checking account to the card. Transfers move money between your accounts without counting as spending, so your budgets stay accurate.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings from the gear icon on the Home screen and choose Delete my account. Your account and all of its data are removed permanently.
