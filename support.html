Install it on your phone

iPhone: open My Money in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link.

My data isn't showing on another device

Make sure you're signed in with the same email on both. Changes made offline sync when you're back online; you can also open Settings and tap Sync now.

How do I log a credit card payment?

Add a Transfer from checking to the card. Transfers don't count as spending.

A balance looks wrong

Open the account (Home > Net worth) and type the real balance from your bank. My Money adds an adjustment so it matches.

Delete my account

Settings > Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all its data.
