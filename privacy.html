My Money

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

My Money ("we", "us") is a personal budgeting website and app. This policy explains what information we keep, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we can't see them.
  • Money information you enter: account names and balances, transactions, budgets, goals, pay schedule and settings. We only have what you type in. We never connect to your bank.

How we use it

Only to run the service for you: to sign you in, save your data and sync it between your devices. We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Connections use HTTPS and stored data is encrypted at rest. Database rules ensure each signed-in person can only read and change their own data. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. Our administrators have technical access to the database for maintenance and security, and we do not look at your financial entries except when you ask us for help or when required by law.

A copy of your data is also kept in your browser so the app works offline. Signing out removes it from that browser.

Using My Money without an account

If you choose "Use without an account", everything stays in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings > Export CSV or Backup.
  • Delete data: Settings > Delete all my data.
  • Delete account: Settings > Delete my account removes your account and all its data permanently.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and to save your data on your device.

Children

My Money is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top.
